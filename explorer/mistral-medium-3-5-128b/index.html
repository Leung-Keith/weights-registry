Mistral-Medium-3.5-128B (2026)

Mistral-Medium-3.5-128B is a large language model released by Mistral AI, released under the Modified MIT license, with a claimed knowledge cutoff of 2026-03-31. Its weights, configuration, and tokenizer files have been cryptographically hashed and are queued for on-chain attestation by Weight Registry.

Description

128B dense model merging what were previously three separate specialist lines (Mistral Medium 3.1 for chat, Magistral for reasoning, Devstral 2 for coding) into one set of weights -- notable both as a rare open-weighting of a 'Medium'-tier flagship and for the merge-not-just-scale design choice. 256k context, accepts text and images.
