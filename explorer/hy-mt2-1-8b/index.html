Hy-MT2-1.8B (2026)

Hy-MT2-1.8B is a large language model released by Tencent, released under the apache-2.0 license, with a claimed knowledge cutoff of 2026-05-21. Its weights, configuration, and tokenizer files have been cryptographically hashed and are queued for on-chain attestation by Weight Registry.

Description

Hy-MT2-1.8B is a compact 1.8B-parameter translation model from Tencent's Hunyuan team, open-sourced May 21, 2026 alongside 7B and 30B-A3B siblings. It supports translation across 33 languages plus Chinese dialect/minority-language pairs, can be quantized to as little as 440MB, and is reported to outperform several mainstream commercial translation APIs despite its size.
