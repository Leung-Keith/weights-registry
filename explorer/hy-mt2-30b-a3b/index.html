Hy-MT2-30B-A3B (2026)

Hy-MT2-30B-A3B is a large language model released by Tencent, released under the apache-2.0 license, with a claimed knowledge cutoff of 2026-05-21. Its weights, configuration, and tokenizer files have been cryptographically hashed and are queued for on-chain attestation by Weight Registry.

Description

Hy-MT2-30B-A3B is Tencent's flagship translation model in the Hy-MT2 family, open-sourced May 21, 2026 alongside 1.8B and 7B siblings. It supports 33 language pairs plus Chinese dialect/minority-language pairs, and Tencent reports it outperforms open models like DeepSeek-V4-Pro and Kimi K2.6 in fast-thinking translation mode.
