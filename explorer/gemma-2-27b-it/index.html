gemma-2-27b-it (2024)

gemma-2-27b-it is a large language model released by Google, released under the gemma license, with a claimed knowledge cutoff of 2024-06-30. Its weights, configuration, and tokenizer files have been cryptographically hashed and are queued for on-chain attestation by Weight Registry.

Description

Gemma 2 27B by Google is an open model built from the same research and technology used to create the Gemini models. Gemma models are well-suited for a variety of text generation tasks and were competitive with much larger proprietary models on release-era leaderboards, using techniques like knowledge distillation and interleaved local/global attention.
