Inside Alibaba's Relentless Qwen Release Cadence

2024-10-02

Alibaba's Qwen team has run one of the fastest release cadences of any major open model family, spanning sizes from half a billion to 72 billion parameters. Qwen2.5-7B, released in September 2024, was a representative entry in that family: a fully open, Apache 2.0-licensed dense model tuned across an unusually broad range of tasks including math and code.

Why it mattered

Qwen's rapid, high-frequency release pattern -- multiple sizes, multiple variants, tight benchmark iteration -- made the Qwen family one of the most widely fine-tuned and deployed open model lineages by 2025, a strategy other issuers have since converged toward. Qwen2.5-7B in particular became a common baseline for comparison in open-model discussions through late 2024 and into 2025.

On the record

  • Issuer: Alibaba
  • License: Apache-2.0
  • Claimed cutoff: 2024-09-18
  • Status: on-chain, attested
