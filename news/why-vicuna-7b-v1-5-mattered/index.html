How Vicuna Turned ChatGPT Logs Into an Open-Source Chatbot Boom

2023-08-15

In early-to-mid 2023, teams discovered that fine-tuning an open base model on real conversational data -- specifically, shared logs of user interactions with ChatGPT -- could produce a chat assistant that felt dramatically more capable than the base model alone, at a tiny fraction of the cost of training from scratch. LMSYS's Vicuna, fine-tuned from Meta's LLaMA on shared ChatGPT conversation logs, became one of the most widely cited examples of this approach.

Why it mattered

Vicuna helped popularize both the "instruction-tuned open chat model" pattern and LMSYS's own Chatbot Arena as a way of benchmarking models by human preference rather than static test sets alone -- an evaluation approach still widely used today. It marks a clear inflection point in the open community's shift from open base models to open chat assistants people would actually want to use.
